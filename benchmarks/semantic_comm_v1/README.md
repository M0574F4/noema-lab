# Semantic-communication benchmark notes

The `local_accounting_case_study*` packs are retained historical research records,
not installed-package examples. They bind private/local dataset manifests and a
workspace-owned training export that are intentionally absent from release archives.
Their checked-in results remain non-publication-ready development evidence; do not
treat the packs as runnable until every referenced dataset and trained artifact is
supplied and revalidated in a new, versioned protocol.

Installed users should start with the packs listed by `noema suite benchmarks
semantic_comm`. Those catalog entries have explicit smoke/experimental/canonical
status and do not depend on an unshipped `.noema` training export.
